milliWorks on the Cardputer

How the screens would look on the device in each milliWorks theme, next to today's firmware (v5). Colours are reduced to the LCD's 16-bit RGB565, the screen is drawn at its real 240 × 135 pixels and scaled up with hard pixels.

Green mask

Keys outlined in copper on the keyboard are the ones this screen responds to. On your keyboard: ← → change the screen, T cycles through the themes.

Sounds: Copper chime

Every theme has its own sound pack with the same eight moments; pick a theme above to switch packs. These are the Cardputer's own sounds, rendered from the firmware code and checked for clipping, clicks and level. Its speaker is small and bright, so on the device they sound thinner than on your laptop.

Focus sounds (Flow app)

Generated on the Cardputer while you work, no music files needed; these loops are rendered from the same code. Start one, then press Mute (or M) to silence it instantly. On the device they fade out at the break.

Breathe guidance (eyes closed)

What tells you the phase without looking. Voice is the default: full quality from the SD card, or the small built-in copy when the card isn't there. Tones use three shapes: up, two taps, down. All are the firmware's own audio.
